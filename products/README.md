## Consumer contract

Product repositories, applications, websites, launchers, packages, and documentation may keep local copies where required for build, deployment, offline use, performance, or platform packaging. Those copies are derivatives and must remain synchronized with the approved source here.

Do not create or approve a new product logo, icon, wordmark, or artwork exclusively inside a consumer repository. Add or revise the canonical asset here first, then synchronize approved derivatives outward.

Historical source repositories may retain artwork for compatibility or packaging, but they are no longer the branding authority for assets represented in this directory.

## Platform-system identities

Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, Glaze UI, and GoreeCloud Mesh are substantive platform systems rather than decorative product badges. Their approved identity artwork belongs under the unified branding repository as it is created or revised, with public claims tied to implemented capabilities and evidence.
